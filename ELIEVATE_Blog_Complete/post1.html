The Beginning: Why I Started

Before there was a finished physique, there was a decision to start.

Every transformation begins with a version of yourself that decides something has to change. For me, fitness became more than simply going to the gym. It became a way to challenge myself and create a stronger version of who I wanted to become.

The early stage can be difficult because the results are not immediate. You can train consistently for weeks and still feel like nothing has changed. That is where patience becomes important. The goal is to build habits that continue even when the mirror does not give you instant feedback.

Start before you feel ready

You do not need the perfect program, the perfect diet or perfect motivation to begin. You need a starting point. Once you begin, you can learn, adjust and improve.

Looking back at an earlier version of myself reminds me that progress is possible when small actions are repeated long enough.
