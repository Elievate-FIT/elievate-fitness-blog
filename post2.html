Training

Building Muscle: The Fundamentals

A strong physique is built through consistent training, recovery and patience.

Building muscle is not about finding a secret exercise. The fundamentals are simple: train consistently, challenge the muscles over time, eat enough to support your goal and recover properly.

Progressive overload

One of the most important ideas in resistance training is progressive overload. Over time, you want your training to become more challenging through additional repetitions, load, better technique or improved control.

Consistency beats intensity

A single great workout cannot replace months of consistent effort. The best training plan is one you can follow long enough to make progress.

Recovery is part of training

Muscle growth does not happen only while you are lifting. Sleep, nutrition and rest give your body the opportunity to recover from training and adapt.
