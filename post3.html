Mindset

Heal. Build. Become.

The biggest transformation is not always the one you can see.

Fitness can change your body, but the process can also change the way you think. Learning to stay disciplined, deal with setbacks and keep working toward a goal creates lessons that reach beyond the gym.

Discipline when motivation fades

Motivation can get you started, but routines help you continue. Some days will feel easy and others will not. Progress comes from continuing through both.

Don't compare your chapter to someone else's

Transformation is personal. Different starting points, schedules and circumstances mean that progress will not look identical for everyone.

Become the person you are working toward

The goal is not simply a before-and-after picture. It is the person you become while putting in the work: more patient, more disciplined and more confident.
